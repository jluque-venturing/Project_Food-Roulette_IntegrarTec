About Us

Discover the story behind Food Roulette

Our Mission

Food Roulette was created with a simple yet powerful mission: to transform the way people discover recipes. We believe that cooking should be an adventure, not a chore. Whether you have leftover ingredients in your fridge or you're looking for inspiration, Food Roulette is here to help you create delicious meals.

Who We Are

We're a passionate team of food enthusiasts and developers dedicated to making recipe discovery fun and accessible to everyone. Our app combines the excitement of a roulette wheel with smart ingredient matching to bring you the perfect recipe every time.

What We Offer

  • Ingredient-Based Search: Tell us what you have, and we'll find the perfect recipe
  • Food Roulette: Let luck decide with our interactive spinning wheel
  • Smart Filtering: Filter by diet type, meal preferences, and cooking time
  • Instant Recommendations: Get recipe suggestions in seconds

Our Vision

We envision a world where nobody has to waste ingredients or struggle with meal planning. Food Roulette is committed to reducing food waste while making cooking more enjoyable and accessible to people of all skill levels.

Made with Love

Every feature, every spin, and every recipe recommendation is built with care and attention to detail. We're constantly improving and adding new features to make your cooking experience even better.
